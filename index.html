ABOUT THE LAB

From sensors to working systems

System Design Lab at NIT Durgapur is organised around hands-on system development. We combine hardware design, embedded programming, sensor integration, signal processing and edge intelligence to build prototypes that can be measured, tested and improved.

HOW WE WORK

Build, test, measure, improve.

Our work starts with a system problem rather than a single algorithm. A typical experiment moves from component selection and circuit integration to firmware, data capture, on-device processing and controlled evaluation. Design decisions are recorded across versions so that changes in hardware and software can be traced clearly.

Practical System Design

Compact experimental platforms that connect sensing, computation and interaction under real device constraints.

Versioned Experiments

V1/V2 development tracks document design changes, implementation choices and the progression of each prototype.

RESEARCH

Research Themes

Our work spans wearable computing, embedded sensing, edge intelligence and multimodal system design, with an emphasis on prototypes that can be tested in the lab and in real settings.

Wearable & Human-Centred Systems

Smart rings, watches, bands and head-worn devices for sensing, interaction and human activity applications.

Embedded System Design

Microcontroller-based hardware/software co-design, low-power sensing and integrated prototype development.

Edge AI & Tiny Intelligence

On-device processing, lightweight inference and intelligent decision-making close to the sensor.

mmWave & Advanced Sensing

Exploration of mmWave and complementary sensor modalities with modern embedded compute platforms.

IoT & Connected Devices

Connected prototypes, telemetry and edge-to-cloud experimentation for smart physical systems.

Experimental Validation

Versioned prototyping, data collection, measurement, performance analysis and iterative system refinement.
